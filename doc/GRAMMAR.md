# Lumina — Reference Grammar

This document provides the exhaustive, normative reference grammar for the Lumina constructed language.

---

## 1. Clause Structure & Word Order

The canonical order of elements in independent and subordinate clauses is strictly:
$$\text{[Subject]} + \text{[Tense/Aspect Markers]} + \text{[Verb]} + \text{[Direct Object]} + \text{[Circumstantial Modifiers]}$$

Examples:
- *Mina sifi la kafa.* = I see the animal.
- *Tina na kidi la kafa kabo.* = You ate the elephant.

---

## 2. The Nominal System

### 2.1 Determiners
- **Definite Article**: `la` preposed to the noun.
  - *la kafa* = the animal
  - *la fapo* = the book
- **Indefinite Article**: Unmarked ($\emptyset$).
  - *kafa* = an animal
  - *fapo* = a book

### 2.2 Pluralization
- **Plural Marker**: Invariable postposition `ro` appended after the noun stem.
  - *kafa ro* = animals
  - *la kafa ro* = the animals
  - *la fapo pa mina ro* = my books

### 2.3 Personal Pronouns
Pronouns derive symmetrically from human domain category `M-` with singular `-na` and plural `-ro`:

| Person | Singular | Plural |
|---|---|---|
| **1st person** (I / We) | `Mina` | `Miro` |
| **2nd person** (You / You all) | `Tina` | `Tiro` |
| **3rd person** (He, She, It / They) | `Lina` | `Liro` |
| **Indefinite / One** | `Masa` | `Masa ro` |

### 2.4 Possession
The genitive particle `pa` expresses ownership or attribution:
- *La fapo pa Mina* = The book of me (My book)
- *La kapa pa Tina* = Your hat
- *La gabo pa Miro* = Our world

---

## 3. The Verbal System

Verbal roots predictably feature first vowel $V_1 = \text{-I-}$ (e.g. *sifi* = to see, *kidi* = to eat, *shili* = to be).  
Verbs are strictly invariant for person and number. Temporal and aspectual distinctions are expressed via preverbal particles.

### 3.1 Tense Markers
- **Present** : `li` (unmarked when context is unambiguous)
  - *Mina li sifi* = I see
- **Past** : `na`
  - *Mina na sifi* = I saw / I have seen
- **Future** : `vo`
  - *Mina vo sifi* = I will see

### 3.2 Aspectual Markers
- **Perfective (Completed action)** : `pe`
  - *Mina pe kidi* = I have finished eating
- **Durative / Continuous (Ongoing action)** : `du`
  - *Mina du kidi* = I am eating (in progress)

### 3.3 Combinations
- *Mina na du kidi* = I was eating (past continuous)
- *Mina na pe kidi* = I had eaten (past perfect)
- *Mina vo du lifi* = I will be moving

---

## 4. Modifiers: Adjectives & Adverbs

### 4.1 Attributive Adjectives
- Always placed **immediately after** the noun they modify.
- Fully invariant in number.
- Canonical form ends in **-u**.
  - *safo sulo* = a beautiful picture
  - *la kafa zuru* = the wild animal
  - *la fapo duru* = the ancient book

### 4.2 Adverbs of Manner
- Derived deterministically by suffixing **-fi** to the adjectival stem:
  - *guru* (large) $\to$ *gurufi* (greatly)
  - *puru* (true) $\to$ *purufi* (truly)
  - *kogu* (clear, bright) $\to$ *kogufi* (clearly)

---

## 5. Negation, Interrogation & Mood

### 5.1 Verbal Negation
The negative particle `ne` is placed immediately before the tense marker or the verb:
- *Mina ne li lifi.* = I do not move.
- *Mina ne na kidi.* = I did not eat.

### 5.2 Polar Interrogation (Yes/No Questions)
Initiate the sentence with the interrogative particle `Ma`:
- *Ma Tina sifi la safo?* = Do you see the picture?
- Answers: `Ya` (Yes) or `No` (No).

### 5.3 Imperative & Injunction
Initiate the directive clause with `Ta`:
- *Ta sifi la fapo!* = Look at the book!
- *Ta ne lifi!* = Do not move!

### 5.4 Evidential & Mood Modifiers
Preposed to the sentence to encode speaker attitude:
- `Sa` : Positive appraisal / relief ("Fortunately...", "It is good that...")
  - *Sa Lina li shili zelu!* = Fortunately he is happy!
- `Fo` : Negative appraisal / regret ("Alas...", "Unfortunately...")
  - *Fo la kafa na kidi Lina.* = Alas the animal ate him.

---

## 6. Prepositions & Connectives

| Particle | Function | Translation | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| `pa` | Direction, location, genitive | to, into, in, of | *pa la gafo* (in the forest) |
| `fa` | Source, origin, ablative | from, origin of | *lifi fa la gabo* (move from the world) |
| `ko` | Instrument, accompaniment | with, on, by means of | *kisi ko la jako* (draw with the pencil) |
| `ve` | Additive coordination | and | *kafa ve masa* (animal and human) |
| `o` | Alternative coordination | or | *kiso o pofo* (inside or outside) |
| `keli` | Relative pronoun | who, that, which | *la kafa keli li lifi* (the animal that moves) |
| `kada` | Temporal conjunction | when, while | *kada Mina shili mito* (when I was a child) |
| `tali` | Consecutive connector | then, therefore | *tali, li piko* (therefore, one understands) |
| `to` | Proximal demonstrative | this | *safo to* (this image) |
| `ta` | Distal demonstrative | that | *safo ta* (that image) |
