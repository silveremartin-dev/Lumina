# Lumina — Beginner's Crash Course

Welcome to **Lumina**, the logical language engineered for mathematical precision and zero ambiguity.

---

## Lesson 1 : The Sound Matrix & Syllable Architecture

### Pure Vowels
In Lumina, letters never change their sound:
- `a` as in *father*
- `e` as in *cafe*
- `i` as in *machine*
- `o` as in *force*
- `u` is always pronounced **"oo"** (as in *flute*)

> **Rule of Stress**: The stress always falls on the **second-to-last syllable**:  
> **KA-fa**, **MI-na**, **SU-lo**.

### The $C_1V_1C_2V_2$ Formula
Every core word is four letters:
- **$C_1$** gives the category domain (`M` = Human, `K` = Physical matter, `F` = Speech, `S` = Vision/Sensation).
- **$V_1$** tells you what part of speech it is:
  - **A** = Noun (`fapo` = book)
  - **I** = Verb (`fipi` = to write)
  - **U** = Adjective (`fupu` = written)
  - **O** = Place/Collective (`fopo` = library)

## Lesson 2 : Pronouns & Strict SVO Syntax

### Personal Pronouns
- `Mina` = I, me
- `Tina` = you (singular)
- `Lina` = he, she, it
- Plurals replace `-na` with `-ro`:
  - `Miro` = we, us
  - `Tiro` = you (plural)
  - `Liro` = they, them

### Articles & Plurals
- To say "the", place `la` **before** the noun: `la kafa` (the animal).
- To make a plural, place `ro` **after** the noun: `la kafa ro` (the animals).
- Without `la`, the noun is indefinite: `kafa` = an animal.

### Simple Sentences
Sentence order is invariant: **Subject - Verb - Object**.
- `Mina sifi la kafa.` = I see the animal.
- `Tina sifi Mina.` = You see me.

## Lesson 3 : Tense & Invariable Conjugation

The verb **never inflects**. Simply insert a small marker immediately before the verb:

| Marker | Tense | Example | English |
|---|---|---|---|
| `li` | Present | `Mina li kidi` | I eat |
| `na` | Past | `Mina na kidi` | I ate / I have eaten |
| `vo` | Future | `Mina vo kidi` | I will eat |
| `du` | In progress | `Mina du kidi` | I am eating |
| `pe` | Completed | `Mina pe kidi` | I have finished eating |

To make a clause negative, place `ne` before the verb:
- `Mina ne na kidi.` = I did not eat.

## Lesson 4 : Questions, Adjectives & Adverbs

### Questions with `Ma`
Begin any polar question with `Ma`:
- `Ma Tina sifi la safo?` = Do you see the picture?
- Answers: `Ya` (Yes) or `No` (No).

### Adjectives Follow the Noun
- `safo sulo` = a beautiful picture (`sulo` = good/beautiful)
- `la kapa suru` = the red hat (`suru` = red)

### Making Adverbs with `-fi`
Add `-fi` to any adjective:
- `puru` (true) $\to$ `purufi` (truly)
- `guru` (large) $\to$ `gurufi` (greatly)

## Lesson 5 : Deconstructing The Little Prince

Here is the famous passage about the boa constrictor in Lumina:

> `La na fisi kafa kofa keli na kidi kafa kabo.`

Word-by-word gloss:
- `La` (it / the book)
- `na` [past]
- `fisi` (show)
- `kafa kofa` (creeping snake animal)
- `keli` (who / that)
- `na` [past]
- `kidi` (eat)
- `kafa kabo` (massive elephant animal).

*Translation:* "It showed a serpent that had eaten an elephant."

And when adults saw the drawing:
> `Ma masa timifi la kapa? Fo no!`  
> *(Does a person fear a hat? Alas no!)*
