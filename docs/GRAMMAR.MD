# Lumina Comprehensive Grammar Specification

**Lumina** is an *a priori* constructed logical language (*conlang*) engineered for total semantic transparency, mathematical predictability, zero morphological irregularities, and algorithmic bidirectional translation.

---

## 1. Foundational Axioms

1. **Zero Morphological Exceptions**: Every grammatical particle, affix, and tense operates universally without irregular stems, suppletion, or portmanteau forms.
2. **Strict SVO Syntax**: Subject - Verb - Object word order is invariable across all declarative and imperative propositions.
3. **Absence of Grammatical Gender**: No noun classes, grammatical genders, or arbitrary agreement categories.
4. **Orthogonal Semantic Matrix ($C_1V_1C_2V_2$)**: Every canonical root consists of exactly four phonemes across two open syllables (CV-CV).
5. **Invariant Penultimate Stress**: Stress universally falls on the penultimate vowel.
6. **Bilingual Symmetry**: Full formal alignment between Lumina, English, and French.

---

## 2. Sentence Structure (SVO Syntax)

The invariant constituent order is:
$$\mathbf{[Subject]} + \mathbf{[Tense/Aspect\ Markers]} + \mathbf{[Verb]} + \mathbf{[Direct\ Object]} + \mathbf{[Prepositional\ Phrases]}$$

### Examples:
- *Mina sifi la kafa.* = I see the animal. / *Je vois l'animal.*
- *Tina na kidi la kafa kabo.* = You ate the elephant. / *Tu as mangé l'éléphant.*
- *Lina vo miva la lvo.* = He will pilot the airplane. / *Il pilotera l'avion.*

## 3. The Nominal System

### 3.1 Articles & Definiteness
* **Definite Article**: `la` placed **before** the noun.
  * *la kafa* = the animal (*l'animal*)
  * *la fapo* = the book (*le livre*)
* **Indefinite Article**: Zero-marked (absence of particle).
  * *kafa* = an animal (*un animal*)
  * *fapo* = a book (*un livre*)

### 3.2 Plural Marker (`ro`)
* Number is not marked on the noun stem. Plurality is indicated by the invariant particle `ro` placed **after** the noun:
  * *kafa ro* = animals (*des animaux*)
  * *la kafa ro* = the animals (*les animaux*)
  * *la gta ro* = the stars (*les étoiles*)

### 3.3 Invariable Personal Pronouns
Pronouns are systematically derived from the human ontological category `M-`:

| Lumina Pronoun | Person | English Equivalent | French Equivalent |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Mina` | 1st person singular | I, me | Je, moi |
| `Tina` | 2nd person singular | You (singular) | Tu, toi |
| `Lina` | 3rd person singular | He, she, it, him, her | Il, elle, lui |
| `Miro` | 1st person plural | We, us | Nous |
| `Tiro` | 2nd person plural | You (plural) | Vous |
| `Liro` | 3rd person plural | They, them | Ils, elles, eux |
| `Masa` | Indefinite / Collective | One, someone, people | On, une personne |

### 3.4 Possession & Genitive (`pa`)
Possession is expressed using the relational particle `pa`:
$$\text{Possessed Item} + \text{pa} + \text{Possessor}$$
* *La fapo pa Mina* = My book (The book of me) / *Mon livre*
* *La kapa pa Tina* = Your hat / *Ton chapeau*
* *La gabo pa Miro* = Our world / *Notre monde*
* *La safo pa la mito* = The child's drawing / *Le dessin de l'enfant*

### 3.5 Proper Nouns
Proper nouns (personal names, places, celestial bodies) retain their capitalized surface form:
* *Mina nomi Silver.* = My name is Silver. / *Je m'appelle Silver.*
* *Lina na voli pa la Arizona ve la Chine.* = He flew to Arizona and China.

## 4. The Verbal System

Verbs are identified by the first vowel $V_1 = \mathbf{i}$ (e.g., `sifi` = to see, `kisi` = to make, `shili` = to be, `fidi` = to say, `nomi` = to be called).

The verb stem is **100% invariant**. It never takes person or number inflections. Tense and aspect are expressed via pre-verbal particles.

### 4.1 Tense Markers
* **Present**: `li` (neutral present, often omitted in clear context)
  * *Mina li sifi* (or *Mina sifi*) = I see / *Je vois*
* **Past**: `na`
  * *Mina na sifi* = I saw / *J'ai vu*
* **Future**: `vo`
  * *Mina vo sifi* = I will see / *Je verrai*

### 4.2 Aspect Markers
* **Perfective (Completed)**: `pe`
  * *Mina pe kidi* = I have finished eating / *J'ai mangé*
* **Durative / Continuous**: `du`
  * *Mina du kidi* = I am eating (be + -ing) / *Je suis en train de manger*
* **Aspect-Tense Combinations**:
  * *Mina na du kidi* = I was eating (past continuous)
  * *Mina na pe kidi* = I had eaten (past perfect)
  * *Mina vo du somo* = I will be sleeping (future continuous)

## 5. Negation, Modality & Questions

### 5.1 Negation (`ne`)
The negative particle `ne` immediately precedes the verb or its tense marker:
* *Mina ne sifi.* = I do not see. / *Je ne vois pas.*
* *Mina ne na kidi.* = I did not eat. / *Je n'ai pas mangé.*
* *Lina ne lifi.* = It does not move. / *Il ne bouge pas.*

### 5.2 Questions (`Ma`)
Polar (Yes/No) questions are formed by placing the interrogative marker `Ma` at the beginning of the clause:
* *Ma Tina sifi la safo?* = Do you see the picture? / *Est-ce que tu vois l'image ?*
* *Ma Tina shili zelu?* = Are you well / How are you? / *Comment vas-tu ?*
* *Ma la kapa timi masa?* = Does a hat scare anyone? / *Pourquoi un chapeau ferait-il peur ?*

### 5.3 Imperative & Directives (`Ta`)
Command or instruction clauses open with the particle `Ta`:
* *Ta sifi la fapo!* = Look at the book! / *Regarde le livre !*
* *Ta ne lifi!* = Do not move! / *Ne bouge pas !*
* *Ta fidi pa Mina.* = Tell me. / *Dis-moi.*

### 5.4 Mood Modifiers (`Sa` / `Fo`)
Sentence-initial attitude particles:
* `Sa` : Positive mood, joy, gratitude (*"Fortunately...", "Good that..."*)
  * *Sa Mina sifi Tina!* = Good to see you! / Hello!
* `Fo` : Negative mood, sorrow, regret (*"Alas...", "Unfortunately..."*)
  * *Fo no, Lina ne shili to.* = Alas no, he is not here.

---

## 6. Modifiers: Adjectives & Derived Adverbs

### 6.1 Qualifying Adjectives
* Placed **strictly after** the noun they modify.
* Invariant across singular and plural.
* Canonical form ends in $V_1 = \mathbf{u}$:
  * *safo sulo* = a beautiful picture
  * *kafa zuru* = a wild animal
  * *fapo duru* = an old book

### 6.2 Regular Adverb Derivation (`-fi`)
Adverbs of manner are deterministically formed by appending **`-fi`** to the adjectival stem:
$$\text{Adverb} = \mathbf{[C_1 U C_2 V_2]\text{-fi}}$$
* `sulo` (good, fine) $\to$ `sulofi` (well, finely)
* `puru` (true) $\to$ `purufi` (truly, in truth)
* `guru` (large, great) $\to$ `gurufi` (greatly, much)
* `zelu` (happy) $\to$ `zelufi` (happily)
* `kogu` (clear) $\to$ `kogufi` (clearly)

## 8. Relational Particles & Conjunctions

| Particle | Function / Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| `pa` | To, towards, for, into, possession | *fidi pa Mina* (tell me), *pa la gafo* (in the forest) |
| `fa` | From, origin, source | *lifi fa la gta* (come from the star) |
| `ko` | With, using, instrument, upon | *kisi ko la jako* (draw with the pencil) |
| `ve` | And (additive coordination) | *kafa ve masa* (animal and human) |
| `o` | Or (alternative coordination) | *kiso o pofo* (inside or outside) |
| `keli` | Who, which, that (relative pronoun) | *la kafa keli na lifi* (the animal that moved) |
| `kada` | When, while (temporal conjunction) | *kada Mina shili mito* (when I was a child) |
| `tali` | Then, so, therefore (consecutive) | *tali Mina na moti* (then I learned) |
| `to` | This (proximal demonstrative) | *safo to* (this drawing) |
| `ta` | That (distal demonstrative) | *safo ta* (that drawing) |
